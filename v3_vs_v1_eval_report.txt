1. WHAT WAS EVALUATED
---------------------
Models   v1       dilstm.pt (6-h) / dilstm_h12.pt (12-h): feat v1, MSE loss, trained on 6,036 gauges
         v3-full  dilstm_v3_full.pt / dilstm_h12_v3_full.pt: feat v3 (5 dynamic + 16 HydroBASINS
                  statics), per-gauge NSE loss (weight cap 25), obs-dropout 0.25, same 6,036 gauges
         persistence  last available observation held flat (baseline)
Both models were trained on 2023-01 .. 2025-06.

Gauges   ALL 9,067 gauges served by the CREST-AI nowcast (nowcast/latest.parquet list).
         Of these, 6,207 have USGS truth in at least one evaluation month; 2,856 have no
         instantaneous discharge in any evaluation month (dead / never-reporting feeds).
         6,020 of the scorable gauges are in the models' training set, 187 are not.

Periods  43 months: January, April, July, October of every year 2016-2026 (2026 through July).
         33 months are OUT-OF-SAMPLE in time (2016-2022, 2025-07 .. 2026-07);
         10 months fall inside the training window (2023-01, -04, -07, -10; 2024-01, -04, -07,
         -10; 2025-01, -04) and are reported separately as IN-SAMPLE.
Issues   EVERY HOUR of every evaluation month = 31,656 issue times x all gauges.

Inputs   Precipitation: hourly MRMS basin means computed with the operational writer's own
         grid/box/summed-area code (bit-identical results) from the same monthly MRMS archives
         the writer and the training prep use (HF vincewin/CREST_data mrms/<Y>/mrms_<Y>_<M>.tar,
         gauge-corrected/Pass-2 grids); 71 hours missing from the tars were filled from
         /media/scratch/ZhiLi/MRMS GaugeCorr tifs; 0 hours missing overall.
         Observations: USGS NWIS instantaneous discharge, downloaded for all 9,067 gauges for
         every month (+4-day lookback, +1-day leads); 23 M rows per month; no lost requests.
         Static attributes: the same gauge_statics table the Space uses.

Obs scenarios (each issue is run three ways, so every gauge is judged in every regime):
         fresh    all observations up to t0 exactly as NWIS holds them (operational best case)
         stale24  observations cut off 24 h before t0 (feed outage / delayed reporting)
         noobs    no observations at all in the 72-h window (dead feed; also the proxy for the
                  2,676 ungauged virtual points served by the dashboard)

Truth    NWIS observation within the trailing hour of t0 + lead (1..6 h for the 6-h family,
         1..12 h for the 12-h family). No interpolation. Sample excluded if no obs in that hour.

Metric   NSE. Primary view: ONE NSE PER GAUGE (all issues and leads of the period pooled),
         then medians / win counts / fraction > 0 across gauges — pooled NSE is dominated by a
         few large rivers and is reported only as a secondary column. Minimum 30 samples per
         gauge. Also: by lead, by month/season, peak ratio max(pred)/max(obs), and a
         leave-one-year-out (LOYO) cross-validation of the routing rule (winner chosen on the
         other 10 years, scored on the held-out year), so the routing gain is out-of-sample.
Fidelity The replay reproduces operations: on the Jul-Aug 2026 archive the replayed v1 matches
         the archived operational v1 predictions with r = 0.997 / NSE = 0.993 (log space).

Reading:
* With fresh observations the two models are equivalent: v1 is 0.003 better at 6 h (wins 63 %
  of gauges), v3-full is 0.002 better at 12 h (wins 53 %). Both clearly beat persistence from
  lead 3 h on (lead 6: 0.945 / 0.943 vs 0.911; lead 12: 0.840 / 0.845 vs 0.771).
* With observations 24 h stale, v3-full is decisively better (+0.15 / +0.20 median NSE, wins at
  86-93 % of gauges; 74 % of gauges keep NSE > 0.5 vs 54 % for v1).
* Without observations, v1 collapses (it predicts near-zero flow: median peak ratio 0.03) and
  is worse than the observed mean at 89 % of gauges; v3-full has positive skill at 64 % of gauges
  (median 0.07, pooled 0.44) but is far from a substitute for a working gauge — its median peak
  ratio is 0.39, i.e. it typically under-predicts peaks by ~60 % from precipitation alone.
* In-sample months (2023-2025) look the same (fresh 6 h 0.975 / 0.973; 12 h 0.934 / 0.936;
  stale24 0.583 / 0.746; noobs -0.308 / 0.166): no memorisation advantage for either model.
* The 187 scorable gauges outside the training set behave the same way (fresh 6 h 0.953 / 0.956,
  12 h 0.884 / 0.900; stale24 6 h 0.486 / 0.648; noobs 6 h -0.399 / -0.066).

4. WHAT IS DEPLOYED (2026-08-18; hysteresis rule 12:40 CDT)
------------------------------------------------------------
* vincewin/CREST_nowcast_model: route_pergauge_v1_v3full.parquet (new file; checkpoints untouched).
* vincewin/CREST_updater scripts/run_nowcast_all.py — gauge-health hysteresis routing:
  - v3-full runs at every gauge every hour; v1 is DISPLAYED at a gauge/horizon family only if
    (a) the table says v1 wins there with fresh obs (winner_{6,12}_fresh == v1) AND
    (b) the gauge is HEALTHY: no observation gap > 12 h for 30 consecutive days.
  - Gap monitor: obs older than 12 h at a run marks the gauge "suspect" (v3 for that hour, streak
    kept — a single dark run can be a lost fetch chunk); dark again next run -> "gap": clean streak
    reset, v3 until data are back AND a full 30-day clean streak has accrued ("probation").
  - Network-wide dark runs (> 80 % of gauges) are treated as our own fetch outage: v3 for that
    hour, gauge streaks frozen (no false probation).
  - Everything else (v3 winners, probation, gap, dead, undecided) -> v3-full.
  - State nowcast/route_state.parquet (gid, clean_since, obs_health) rewritten hourly in
    CREST_data; bootstrapped from the 670 archived runs 2026-07-21..08-18 (5,773 clean streaks,
    3,294 in gap). latest.parquet carries model / model12 / obs_health / obs_clean_since columns
    and metadata route_rule, n_healthy, n_probation, n_gap, n_suspect, net_outage.
  - Because the archive only reaches back 28 days, no gauge can prove a 30-day clean month until
    ~2026-08-20; until then all gauges show v3-full (fresh-obs v1/v3 is a tie, so no skill cost).
* The 2,676 ungauged virtual points stay on v3-full (the no-obs regime, where v3-full beats v1
  at 92 % of gauges) — with the caveat that no-obs skill is modest and weakest in fall/West.

5. CAVEATS
----------
* NSE per gauge over a whole month (or 43 months) has a large truth variance in the denominator,
  so absolute values (0.97) are much higher than the 22-day summer replay reported earlier
  (0.46); the two are not comparable, only rankings are.
* Historical NWIS data are approved/revised values; operational obs are provisional and arrive
  with lag. The "fresh" scenario is therefore a best case; the stale24 scenario brackets delays.
* MRMS product changes over 2016-2026 (GaugeCorr -> MultiSensor Pass 2) are inside the eval;
  results are stable across years (fig12), so no product-era effect is visible.
* No-obs results apply to gauged basins with obs withheld; ungauged virtual points additionally
  lack the per-basin statics quality of the gauge table (median-filled where missing).

7. PATTERNS vs GAGES-II ATTRIBUTES (added 2026-08-18; nowcast_eval/multiyear/gages2_patterns.txt,
   pergauge_with_gages2.csv; map fig13_noobs_skill_and_persistence_gain_maps.png)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Join of the 6,211 decided gauges with GAGES-II (class, ecoregion, HUC2, area, climate, snow,
BFI, dams, land cover, soils, disturbance).  Out-of-sample months, 6-h family unless noted.

Fresh obs (v1 vs v3 nearly a coin flip, |median dNSE| <= 0.003):
  v3 wins more at 12 h in small basins (<200 km2: 62-65 %), urban (>35 % developed: 67 %,
  dNSE +0.011), permeable/groundwater basins (perm >8 in/hr: 77 %; BFI>60: 66 %), Great Lakes
  HUC04 (70 %), Great Basin HUC16 (69 %), snow>50 % (64 %). v1 wins large rivers (>5,000 km2:
  63-69 %) and the humid East Highlands / SE Plains (v3 only 24-34 %).
Stale-24h obs (v3 wins 86-93 % everywhere; magnitude of v3 skill is geographic):
  v3 NSE ~0.9 in snowmelt (>50 % snow 0.95), groundwater (BFI>60 0.91), big rivers (>20k km2
  0.91), HUC09/14/16/17 (~0.9); collapses in flashy warm rain basins: HUC15 0.22, HUC12 0.47,
  urban>35 % 0.43, BFI<30 0.53, WestPlains 0.50. v1 wins only in slow northern/regulated
  systems (MxWdShld 22 %, HUC09 31 %, dam storage 500-5000 33 %).
No obs (strongest geography = runoff-generation mechanism):
  positive skill in humid rain-driven East + Pacific coast (EastHghlnds 85 % of gauges NSE>0,
  SEPlains 83 %, NorthEast 83 %, HUC06 93 %; PPT>=100 cm 79-80 % vs <70 cm 37-45 %; rho PPT
  +0.45, T_avg +0.31); negative in snow/energy-driven West interior and northern Plains (HUC16
  22 %, 10U 29 %, HUC09 9 %, MxWdShld 27 %, snow>50 % 24 %; rho elev -0.35, snow -0.23), in
  groundwater/karst/wetland systems (BFI>60 40 %; Florida peninsula), and on big rivers (>5,000
  km2 30-34 %, median -0.05) vs small basins (<50 km2 80 %, median 0.17); urban basins are the
  best no-obs case (>35 % developed 93 % positive, median 0.26). The 8 % of gauges where v1
  "wins" without obs lie in the same failing regions (both models fail; v1's flat-line is nearer
  the mean).
Nowcast vs persistence (fresh obs): gains >=0.05 concentrate in Appalachians/Piedmont/Gulf
  coast, Pacific coast, Texas Hill Country and metro areas (small, warm, rain-driven; rho T_avg
  +0.32, area -0.32, BFI -0.32, snow -0.28); ~persistence across northern Plains, Rockies and
  large rivers; 435 gauges worse than persistence, clustered in regulated / snowmelt /
  diurnal-cycle basins.
Implications: routing already exploits these regimes; the ungauged (grey) points deserve a
  regime-based confidence tag (fig13 top is that mask); the no-obs weakness in the West calls
  for temperature/snowpack (or longer memory) in a future v4, not more precipitation features.
